MELLIZAS

Italian DJ duo · Sonia & Sara
Tech House · Latin Tech · Afro House

InstagramTikTokSpotifyYouTubeSoundCloudBeatportApple MusicDownload Press Kit & Photos

Biography

MELLIZAS is the Italian DJ duo of twin sisters Sonia and Sara. They grew up sharing a love of music and club culture. Behind the decks, they bring together the drive of Tech House, Latin rhythms and Afro House percussion, shaping each set around the crowd in front of them.

Performances

The duo has played clubs, festivals and beach events across the Canary Islands, mainland Spain, Italy and Morocco. Their press kit also lists appearances in Malta and the Dominican Republic, with highlights including Eywa Festival, Café del Mar and Hard Rock Café Madrid.

Selected releases

“Oye Que Vuelta” with Edu Campos, “No Digas Nada”, “Coquetea”, “Bring the Bass Line” and “Afro Parade”.

ARTIST / 01

MR. MAUI

Gran Canaria, Spain · Active since 2022
Afro House / Organic House / Afro Latino · 120–125 BPM

Biography

A Canary Islands DJ and producer with a background in live drumming and percussion, Mr. Maui builds his sound around organic textures, tribal rhythms and Atlantic warmth. His work brings electronic and organic music together through immersive events and DJ sets.

Stages & collaborations

His appearances include Blue Marlin Ibiza Sky Lounge’s Amazona residency, Selvática, Tropicalia, Papagayo, Hanoi Club and BAKKARA in Las Palmas, with international performances in Africa. He has shared lineups with DJ Chus, G. Zamora, M.E.M.O., Aaron Sevilla, Macarena Hoffman, Wildness Rose and Claudia León.

Labels & releases

His catalogue includes MIAMI 305 RECORDS, Café de Anatolia, YHV Records, Caelum Records, Exx Chill, Selvática Records, Batuque Records and Native Music. Featured releases: UMAII, Shadow, Maobak, Ngona and Under The Moonlight.

ARTIST / 03

KARO GOMEZ

Cartagena de Indias, Colombia · Based in Colombia
DJ / Producer · A&R
House · Deep House · Tech House · Latin House · Afro House · Afro Tech

Biography

Karo Gomez’s musical journey began in 1992, at the age of eleven, through Latin percussion. By sixteen she was playing congas in Cartagena de Indias’ alternative rock scene. Those rhythmic roots evolved into electronic music, combining live percussion sensibility with contemporary beats.

Her seven years in Buenos Aires helped shape her production career. In 2008, she developed her technical skills at Escuela Sónica and took private production classes with Hernán Paredes. In the booth, she uses mixers and controllers to build vibrant atmospheres that connect music, energy and emotion.

Stages & collaborations

She has shared stages and DJ booths with Dondo, Obando, Santiago Fos, Victoria Angel, July Aristy, Dier and Moreno & Prieto. Her EPK features events in Miami, Barcelona, Cartagena, Medellín and Argentina, alongside annual appearances across Argentina, Europe and the United States.

Labels & projects

MIAMI 305 RECORDS.

Based in Colombia, she leads IMUS ARTIST, her music production and image consultancy company, in partnership with producer Juan Diazo. As A&R for Miami 305, she contributes to discovering and developing new talent.

Technical rider

Pioneer V10 mixer, CDJ-3000 players and RMX effects. Production requirements can be coordinated with the booking team.

ARTIST / 04

MISS M

Puerto Rico · Also known as Lisa M
DJ since 2005
Afro Latin House · AfroTech House · Tech House

Biography

Miss M, famously known as Lisa M, is a trailblazer in Latin Urban Music from Puerto Rico. Her sound spans Afro Latin House, AfroTech House and Tech House. Since beginning her DJ journey in 2005, she has captivated audiences with a style rooted in Caribbean rhythm and an instinctive connection to the dancefloor.

Miami became her launching pad, connecting her with international DJs and clubs. Her career has included Café Olé in Spain and residencies at Club Space Ibiza, Pacha and Discoteque in Barcelona.

Artistry & sound

A singer, songwriter, audio engineer, music producer and radio host, Miss M also runs her own record label, Pionera Music. She blends percussion into a distinctive Caribbean tribal sound, pairing it with carefully selected tracks to make every session a memorable experience.

Driven by her passion for music, she continues to evolve through each project, pushing musical boundaries and keeping audiences eager for what comes next.

Labels

MIAMI 305 RECORDS, Pionera Music.

ARTIST / 05

EDWARD MAYA

Romania
DJ · Producer · Songwriter
Electronic Dance Music

Biography

Edward Maya is a Romanian DJ, producer, songwriter and musician whose international breakthrough came in 2009 with “Stereo Love”, featuring Vika Jigulina. Its distinctive accordion melody and fusion of Eastern European influences with electronic dance music introduced his sound to audiences around the world.

Music & career

A Billboard Music Award winner and multi-platinum artist, he followed “Stereo Love” with releases including “This Is My Life” and “Desert Rain”. His work combines memorable melodies with dancefloor rhythms, alongside collaborations with artists such as Timmy Trumpet and Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike.

Labels & projects

MIAMI 305 RECORDS · Mayavin Records.

Beyond performing and producing, his Studio House network brings together music publishing, radio, education and events, reflecting his interest in supporting emerging talent.

MANUELA
GANDOLFO

Italy · Based in Ibiza
DJ & Producer · Since 2008
Afro House · Organic House · Lounge

Biography

Manuela Gandolfo is an Italian DJ and producer from Turin, based in Ibiza. Her journey began in 2008, playing techno on vinyl. Over the years, her musical identity has evolved into a blend of Afro House, Organic House and Lounge, with melodic textures and an elegant approach to selection.

After moving to Ibiza in 2017, she became a guest on Anna Tur’s Ibiza Global Radio show. From 2021 to 2025, she held a residency at Amàre Beach Hotel, shaping a sound that moves between relaxed listening and dancefloor energy.

Performances

Her Ibiza appearances include Space’s We Love Sunday, Bora Bora, Tantra, Lolas, Jarana, Space Eat and Dance, Nui, IT, Vybe, Nassau Beach Club and Sunkeys. Her international journey has taken her to Paris, London, Sofia, Vienna, Zanzibar and Thailand, with appearances at City Hall in Barcelona, Ikarus during Florence Fashion Week, Le Diskrete in Paris, Nungwe Club in Zanzibar and Rainforest in Thailand.

Radio

Manuela runs Changeover Radio Show. Her radio appearances include Ibiza Sonica, Ibiza Global Radio, Pure Ibiza, Playasol, Miami Radio, It’s All About the Music and Peninsula.

Selected releases

Her catalogue includes “Kufuna”, “Chandra”, “Yua”, a remix of “Wīīru”, “Humans Need Freedom”, “Ninaimba”, “Lifetime” featuring Macchiavelli and “Arcadia” featuring Francesca Lombardo. Explore her music through the listening links above.

Labels

MIAMI 305 RECORDS, Switch Records, Café de Anatolia, Groove Society, Camel VIP, Loop Jewels, Klaphouse and Ecoe.

Technical rider

Two Pioneer CDJ-3000 players and one Pioneer DJM-900 mixer. Event production details can be coordinated with the booking team.
