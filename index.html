ARTIST / 01

Photo: Twentysix Agency

MR. MAUI

Gran Canaria, Spain · Active since 2022
Afro House / Organic House / Afro Latino · 120–125 BPM

Biography

A Canary Islands DJ and producer with a background in live drumming and percussion, Mr. Maui builds his sound around organic textures, tribal rhythms and Atlantic warmth. His work brings electronic and organic music together through immersive events and DJ sets.

Stages & collaborations

His appearances include Blue Marlin Ibiza Sky Lounge’s Amazona residency, Selvática, Tropicalia, Papagayo, Hanoi Club and BAKKARA in Las Palmas, with international performances in Africa. He has shared lineups with DJ Chus, G. Zamora, M.E.M.O., Aaron Sevilla, Macarena Hoffman, Wildness Rose and Claudia León.

Labels & releases

His catalogue includes Café de Anatolia, YHV Records, Caelum Records, Exx Chill, Selvática Records, Batuque Records and Native Music. Featured releases: UMAII, Shadow, Maobak, Ngona and Under The Moonlight.

Worldwide representation: Twentysix Agency.

ARTIST / 02

Photo: Twentysix Agency

ELI ROJAS

Ibiza, Spain · Colombia · Active since 2015
Latin Tech · House · Techno · Afro-Latin rhythms

Biography

Colombian DJ and producer Eli Rojas brings her cultural roots to electronic music, combining Colombian rhythms with house, techno and Afro-Latin influences. Based in Ibiza, her connection with Blue Marlin Ibiza has shaped a sound built for the island’s dancefloors and international audiences.

Stages & collaborations

Her international appearances include Tomorrowland and Thailand’s Jungle Experience. She has featured on Groove Box with Sam Divine and collaborated with producers Massianello and Juany Bravo.

Labels

Her artist profile highlights Toolroom, Make The Girls Dance and Club Sweat.

Worldwide representation: Twentysix Agency.
